Software & Web Development

Builds the web interfaces customers use to configure and order vehicles.

E-Commerce UX/UI Front-End Developer

Salary

About the role

What this role does and why it exists.

Design and develop responsive web interfaces for the digital retail platform. Combine visual prototyping with front-end engineering to optimise the vehicle purchasing journey.

Key responsibilities

What you will do, listed in priority order.

  1. Create wireframes and interactive prototypes in Figma for vehicle configurations.
  2. Build responsive web interfaces for vehicle configuration and online ordering.
  3. Maintain web accessibility standards (WCAG) across all customer-facing retail pages.
  4. Implement loading states and performance budgets to optimise page speed.
  5. Integrate interfaces with order, payment and inventory APIs, minimising third-party dependencies.

Essential requirements

What you must have to be considered.

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field.
  • Minimum 3 years of commercial experience in front-end development or UX/UI design.
  • Expert-level proficiency in Figma.
  • Strong proficiency in semantic HTML5, CSS3, and core JavaScript.
  • Practical knowledge of web accessibility standards and ARIA practices.

Preferable criteria

A bonus, but not required.

  • Professional working proficiency in English (IELTS 6.0 equivalent or higher).
  • Experience with modern front-end frameworks (such as React or Vue.js).
  • Familiarity with secure data handling practices for online checkout systems.
  • Previous experience in e-commerce or automotive digital retail platforms.

What success looks like in this role.

  • 90-day goal

    Raise vehicle-configurator completion rate by 15%.
